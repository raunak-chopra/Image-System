## Requirements research

Registered SRC-32 through SRC-39 after reading the relevant official pages: IBM visual taxonomy/layout, Apple product-page presentation, Google Play asset/icon rules, Android adaptive icons, Carbon empty-state patterns, Google uploaded-display specs, Pinterest creative hierarchy and YouTube thumbnail help. The [source register](source-register.md) records exactly what was read and which linked detail pages failed.

Key production implications: UI screenshots must be captured from the real app; launcher layers and store icons are different resources; empty/no-results/error states need different semantics; fixed display slots need actual-size recomposition; native controls, exact marks, data charts and print masters require the corresponding native production process. Requirements were paraphrased; no official example assets, code or page prose were copied. These constraints do not establish the compatibility or quality of an image-generation model.

Meta's ad-guide fetch returned a login/temporary-block page, so no fresh Meta numeric requirement was asserted. Existing Meta format values remain dated historical guidance and must be verified for a launch. Apple HIG's app-icon detail was a JavaScript shell; its unsupported detailed constraints were not invented. Google Play screenshot dimensions remain device/console-specific instead of assigning one universal size.
