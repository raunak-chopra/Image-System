# Editorial food asset prompt pack

## Shared locks

- Real editorial food photography; appetizing, believable, and tactile.
- Warm but natural light; matte ceramics, linen, wood, paper, and fresh ingredients should retain realistic texture.
- Warm paper-and-ink mood means a restrained cream, oat, ochre, clay, olive, and russet palette with subtle natural film grain—not an illustration, paper collage, or graphic print.
- Keep the main food subject centered in the lower two-thirds. Reserve the upper 28–32% as calm, lightly textured negative space for a title overlay; do not place food, hands, or high-contrast props in that area.
- No typography, logo, watermark, UI, collage, split screen, borders, stickers, readable packaging, or restaurant-menu styling.
- Avoid glossy commercial food-ad styling, plastic-looking food, excessive props, impossible ingredients, floating objects, and center-crowded clutter.

LOCKED ELEMENTS: Real food photography; believable portions and ingredient textures; matte ceramics; tactile wood, linen, and paper surfaces; no people or hands; no invented branding or readable labels.

SCENE / COMPOSITION: 35-degree tabletop camera with a natural 50mm editorial look.
