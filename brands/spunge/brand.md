## Product and packaging locks
- Bottle: off-white matte rounded-rectangular container with flip-top lid.
- Logo: vertical lowercase `spunge®` wordmark along the edge.
- Pairing the bottle with the silver-foil embossed monocarton outer box elevates luxury.

## Visual do
- Ground premium containers on architectural pedestals (stone, matte ceramic, concrete plinth) with soft contact shadows.
- Translucent golden-amber softgels with real caustics and clean oil/water droplets; glowing micro-emulsion dispersion to show absorption.
- Simple side-by-side benchmark graphics (e.g. standard vs advanced) beat narrative text.
- Feature callouts as enclosed circular or pill badges with high-contrast glyphs.
- 16:9 split hierarchy: product on one side, structured typography in open negative space on the other.

## Visual don't
- Floating bottles with no surface or contact shadow; center-crowded clutter.
- Generic wellness imagery (yoga at sunset, floating leaves, generic smiling models) and hyperbolic wording.
- Off-palette neon colors.
- Dense fine print inside the raster model: in-model text is limited to bold headlines, short badges and one-line footnotes.

## Amazon defaults for this brand
- 1:1 gallery tiles; 16:9 for Brand Store headers and premium A+ modules.
- Left product hero plus right feature-card stack; clinical white or subtle oceanic gradient.
