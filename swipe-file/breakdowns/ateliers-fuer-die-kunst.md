# Ateliers fur die Kunst (arts association identity)

- **Source:** https://www.behance.net/gallery/227513649/Ateliers-fuer-die-Kunst (Behance, reference from owner's `Behance V2.docx`)
- **Category:** non-profit identity / motion system
- **Best-fit formats:** web-hero; OOH poster; stationery
- **Evidence reviewed:** text + hero image

## Breakdown

- **Layout and grid:** Business cards floating above a blue cloudy sky, shot from above at angles; modular geometric colored shapes defined as a system (folded, layered planes).
- **Text hierarchy:** Lowercase wordmark with small descriptor; ample empty sky.
- **Type:** Lowercase geometric sans wordmark.
- **Color logic:** Soft sky blues with colored geometric accents.
- **Imagery:** Surreal floating stationery on cloud sky; signage and poster applications.
- **Hook:** 'Art needs space to unfold' made literal: objects floating in open space.
- **Why it works:** One concept expressed in photography, geometry and motion alike.
