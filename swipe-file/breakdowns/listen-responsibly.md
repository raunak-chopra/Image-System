# #ListenResponsibly (Budweiser print/OOH)

- **Source:** https://www.behance.net/gallery/241582929/ListenResponsibly (Behance, reference from owner's `Behance V2.docx`)
- **Category:** advertising campaign (responsible drinking)
- **Best-fit formats:** meta-feed-4x5; meta-story-9x16; OOH
- **Evidence reviewed:** text + title card

## Breakdown

- **Layout and grid:** Dark, near-black concert-atmosphere frames, red brand accents, centered logo and hashtag.
- **Text hierarchy:** Brand mark + campaign hashtag, then short poetic lines; copy-led, emotion over product.
- **Type:** Condensed bold hashtag; small tracked caption lines.
- **Color logic:** Black and brand red.
- **Imagery:** Unrepeatable concert moments (per text); tone is presence, not excess.
- **Hook:** 'You don't miss a single note' - presence as the benefit.
- **Why it works:** Moment-led emotional hook; brand sits quietly in the corner of the frame.
