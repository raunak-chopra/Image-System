# Archivio - Coffee & Cultura branding

- **Source:** https://www.behance.net/gallery/241856329/Archivio-Coffee-Cultura-Branding (Behance, reference from owner's `Behance V2.docx`)
- **Category:** cafe / retail branding
- **Best-fit formats:** organic social; packaging; web
- **Evidence reviewed:** text + 20 images in contact grid

## Breakdown

- **Layout and grid:** Mockup grid: bags, cups, menu cards, shopfront, stamps and tote on a pale butter-yellow field; mix of flat packshots and in-hand lifestyle crops.
- **Text hierarchy:** Small handwritten-style wordmark with a stamp variant; copy is sparse, notes and marks do the talking.
- **Type:** Handwritten script wordmark plus typewriter-style small text; handwritten annotations used as accents only.
- **Color logic:** Warm butter yellow, cream and taupe; very restrained; photography is neutral and white-dominant.
- **Imagery:** Barista hands, takeaway cups and paper bags, croissant and plate details, white studio packshots for the same items.
- **Hook:** The brand as a personal archive: notes, highlights, stamps.
- **Why it works:** Whole identity runs on one idea (annotation) and one soft palette, so many applications feel unified.
