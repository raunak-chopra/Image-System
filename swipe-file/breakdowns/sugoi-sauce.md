# SUGOI - sauce brand identity & packaging

- **Source:** https://www.behance.net/gallery/251414151/sugoi-sauce-brand-identity-packaging (Behance, reference from owner's `Behance V2.docx`)
- **Category:** food packaging / brand identity
- **Best-fit formats:** meta-feed-4x5; amazon-gallery; shelf
- **Evidence reviewed:** text + 6 images (3 photos, 3 color/logo tiles)

## Breakdown

- **Layout and grid:** Triptych hero: lifestyle portrait | product in produce net on saturated yellow | pour shot on white. Flat color tiles with centered wordmark as palette chips.
- **Text hierarchy:** Huge condensed display wordmark and flavor name dominate the label; usage line DIP / MARINADE / GLAZE as a small tertiary band.
- **Type:** Loud heavy display sans, all caps, wide tracking on the usage line; one typeface family.
- **Color logic:** Flavor color-coding: each SKU owns one saturated color (red, orange, lime/yellow); background of each hero matches or contrasts the SKU color; white clean backdrops for pour/use shots.
- **Imagery:** Product held to camera by a person in streetwear; ingredient-as-set-dressing (chilies in net); in-use pour over food with squeeze motion.
- **Hook:** Flavor identity in one glance: color = flavor; always show the product in a moment of use.
- **Why it works:** Instantly scannable range, strong shelf and thumbnail presence, consistent system across lifestyle, ingredient and use shots.
