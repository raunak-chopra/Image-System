# Eight & Bob (niche fragrance bottle design)

- **Source:** https://www.behance.net/gallery/250590647/Eight-Bob (Behance, reference from owner's `Behance V2.docx`)
- **Category:** fragrance / luxury product
- **Best-fit formats:** amazon-gallery; meta-feed-4x5; web-hero
- **Evidence reviewed:** text + first hero image

## Breakdown

- **Layout and grid:** Single bottle low in frame, angled, standing on the edge of a pale step with a long soft shadow on a light gray gradient; generous negative space above.
- **Text hierarchy:** Product alone; label small; mood from light and shadow, not text.
- **Type:** Understated serif/sans label type (per text: Art Deco influence, understated luxury).
- **Color logic:** Neutral gray/white studio with amber liquid as the only color.
- **Imagery:** Glass bottle with warm liquid, soft raking light, caustic glow at the base.
- **Hook:** Understated luxury through restraint.
- **Why it works:** Negative space and one warm accent create premium feel without props.
