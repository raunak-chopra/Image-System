# SALTED (marine skincare packaging)

- **Source:** https://www.behance.net/gallery/249227989/SALTED (Behance, reference from owner's `Behance V2.docx`)
- **Category:** skincare packaging / branding
- **Best-fit formats:** amazon-gallery; amazon-aplus; meta-feed-4x5
- **Evidence reviewed:** text + first hero image and 2 partial images

## Breakdown

- **Layout and grid:** Hero: full range standing on a rock plinth against a deep blue gradient sky; macro crops of cartons and foam texture; typographic plate.
- **Text hierarchy:** Product names and key info clear, supporting details small; layouts standardized across all formats and sizes.
- **Type:** Clean contemporary sans with strict hierarchy (per text); one display plate with tall condensed serif/sans.
- **Color logic:** Coral-reef-derived palette: blue, coral orange, pink, green - each product its own hue against a marine blue environment.
- **Imagery:** Product range grouped on natural stone; wet/foam texture macros; ocean as narrative element connecting product, formulation and image.
- **Hook:** Origin story made visual: sea + stone + hue-coded range.
- **Why it works:** Range cohesion through one environment and one color logic; texture macros sell the formulation.
